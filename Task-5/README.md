# Task 5: SQL-Based Analysis of Product Sales

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing product sales data using **SQL and MySQL Workbench**.

The analysis uses the **Chinook Database**, a relational database containing information about artists, albums, tracks, customers, invoices, and sales transactions.

The main objective is to demonstrate how SQL can be used to extract meaningful business insights from relational data through table relationships, joins, aggregations, filtering, sorting, and business calculations.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze product sales performance.
- Identify top-selling products.
- Calculate total sales revenue.
- Analyze revenue by country.
- Examine monthly sales performance.
- Analyze revenue by artists and genres.
- Identify the highest-value customers.
- Demonstrate the use of SQL `JOIN`s.
- Apply aggregation functions to generate business metrics.
- Use a window function to rank products by revenue.

---

##️ Database Structure

The main relationships used for the sales analysis are:

```text
Artist
   ↓
Album
   ↓
Track
   ↓
InvoiceLine
   ↓
Invoice
   ↓
Customer
```

### Important tables

| Table | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `Artist` | Contains artist information |
| `Album` | Contains album information |
| `Track` | Contains individual products/tracks |
| `Genre` | Contains music genre information |
| `Customer` | Contains customer information |
| `Invoice` | Contains customer invoices and billing information |
| `InvoiceLine` | Contains individual purchased products, quantities, and prices |

The `InvoiceLine` table is particularly important because it provides the product-level sales information used to calculate revenue and units sold.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Total Revenue

Calculated the overall revenue generated from all sales transactions.

**Business Question:**

> What is the total revenue generated by the business?

---

### 2. Top-Selling Products

Identified the products generating the highest revenue.

**Business Question:**

> Which products are the best performers in terms of revenue?

The analysis also examined products based on **units sold** to distinguish sales volume from revenue performance.

---

### 3. Revenue by Country

Analyzed sales revenue according to customer billing country.

**Business Question:**

> Which countries generate the highest sales revenue?

This provides a geographic view of business performance.

---

### 4. Monthly Sales Performance

Analyzed revenue by month to identify changes in sales performance over time.

**Business Questions:**

- Which months generate the highest revenue?
- Which months have lower sales?
- Are there noticeable patterns in monthly performance?

---

### 5. Top Artists by Revenue

Combined artist, album, track, and sales information to identify the artists generating the highest revenue.

**Business Question:**

> Which artists contribute the most to overall revenue?

---

### 6. Revenue by Genre

Analyzed revenue generated by different music genres.

**Business Question:**

> Which genres are the strongest contributors to sales?

---

### 7. Average Order Value

Calculated the average value of customer invoices.

**Business Question:**

> What is the average amount spent per order?

---

### 8. Top Customers

Identified customers who generated the highest total spending.

**Business Question:**

> Which customers are the most valuable based on total spending?

---

## Bonus: Window Function

A SQL **`RANK()` window function** was used to rank products according to their total revenue.

This demonstrates an advanced SQL technique for creating rankings without losing the underlying grouped data.

**Business Question:**

> How can products be ranked according to their revenue performance?

## SQL JOINs Used

The project demonstrates relationships between multiple tables, including:

- Artist → Album
- Album → Track
- Track → InvoiceLine
- Invoice → InvoiceLine
- Customer → Invoice
- Genre → Track

These relationships allow product-level sales information to be combined with customer, artist, album, genre, and geographic information.
